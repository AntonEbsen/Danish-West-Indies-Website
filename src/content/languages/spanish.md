---
Due to geographical proximity, there was significant historical migration between the Danish West Indies and neighboring Spanish-speaking islands, particularly Puerto Rico (including Vieques and Culebra). Spanish is widely spoken across the Caribbean today, making it a critical language for regional accessibility and pan-Caribbean historical research.
