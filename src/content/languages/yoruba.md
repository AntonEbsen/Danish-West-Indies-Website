---
title: "Yorùbá"
code: "yo"
region: "West Africa (Nigeria/Benin)"
description: "A major West African language representing the diverse origins of the enslaved."
---
While the Danes had forts on the Gold Coast, the transatlantic slave trade was expansive. Many enslaved individuals brought to the Danish West Indies originated from the Bight of Benin and surrounding regions where Yorùbá is spoken. Including Yorùbá acknowledges the diverse, multi-ethnic African origins of the Caribbean diaspora.
