---
title: "Français (French)"
code: "fr"
region: "Caribbean / French West Indies"
description: "The language of neighboring colonial powers and a major linguistic influence in the region."
---
The French had a massive colonial presence in the Caribbean (Haiti, Guadeloupe, Martinique). There was constant trade, smuggling, and movement of people (both free and enslaved) between the Danish West Indies and French territories. French support acknowledges the interconnected nature of the Caribbean colonial economy.
