English is the default language of the digital memorial because it is the primary language spoken today in the U.S. Virgin Islands (St. Croix, St. Thomas, and St. John). Making the archives available in English is a crucial step in ensuring that the descendants of those enslaved in the Danish West Indies can access, read, and understand the historical records of their ancestors without language barriers.
