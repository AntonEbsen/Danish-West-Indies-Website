---
title: "Nederlands (Dutch)"
code: "nl"
region: "Caribbean / Early Colonial Elite"
description: "The dominant language of the early planter class on St. Thomas and St. John."
---
In the early colonial period, Dutch planters were so numerous on St. Thomas and St. John that Dutch, rather than Danish, became the dominant language of the white elite. This profound Dutch influence laid the grammatical and lexical foundation for Negerhollands, the creole language spoken by the enslaved population on those islands.
