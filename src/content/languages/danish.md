title: "Dansk (Danish)"
code: "da"
region: "Denmark / Danish West Indies"
description: "The language of the colonizers and the primary language of the colonial archives."
---
Danish is the source language for the vast majority of the colonial records held at the Danish National Archives (Rigsarkivet). Providing the memorial in Danish is essential for researchers in Denmark and serves as a vital educational tool for the Danish public, fostering a direct confrontation with the nation's colonial past and its role in the transatlantic slave trade.
