---
title: "Kikongo"
code: "kg"
region: "Central Africa (Kongo)"
description: "Representing the massive number of people taken from Central Africa."
---
A significant portion of the enslaved people brought to the Americas, including the Danish West Indies, were captured from the Loango Coast and the Kingdom of Kongo in Central Africa. Kikongo honors this Central African heritage, which deeply influenced Caribbean culture, religion, and resistance movements.
