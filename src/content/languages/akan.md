---
title: "Akan (Twi)"
code: "ak"
region: "West Africa (Ghana)"
description: "A primary language of the Gold Coast, the origin point for many enslaved individuals."
---
The Danish transatlantic slave trade operated heavily out of forts on the "Gold Coast" (modern-day Ghana), most notably Fort Christiansborg in Accra. A massive percentage of the enslaved people brought to the Danish West Indies were captured from this region. Including Akan (Twi) honors the specific West African origins and cultural heritage of the ancestors.
