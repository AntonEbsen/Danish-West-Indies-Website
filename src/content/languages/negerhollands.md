---
title: "Negerhollands"
code: "nh"
region: "St. Thomas & St. John"
description: "The Dutch-based creole language historically spoken by the enslaved population."
---
Negerhollands was the primary language of the enslaved people on St. Thomas and St. John. Born from the interaction between West African languages and the Dutch spoken by early planters, it is a profound testament to survival and cultural synthesis. While now considered an extinct first language, honoring it here restores a vital piece of linguistic heritage to the memorial.
