title: "Svenska (Swedish)"
code: "sv"
region: "Scandinavia / St. Barthélemy"
description: "The language of a neighboring colonial power and modern Scandinavian researchers."
---
Sweden held the nearby island of Saint Barthélemy from 1784 to 1878. As a free port, St. Barts saw massive trade and movement of enslaved people connected to the Danish West Indies. Today, Swedish is included to support Scandinavian researchers who frequently collaborate on the colonial history of the region.
