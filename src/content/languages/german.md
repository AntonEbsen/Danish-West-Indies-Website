---
The Moravian Brethren (Herrnhuters), a German-speaking missionary group, arrived in the Danish West Indies in the 1730s. They were among the first Europeans to closely interact with the enslaved population. Because they kept meticulous diaries and census records, a vast amount of the primary source material and early historiography regarding the islands is written in German.
