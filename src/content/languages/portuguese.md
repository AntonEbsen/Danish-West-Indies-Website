Portugal initiated the transatlantic slave trade, and their linguistic footprint is embedded in creoles and histories across the Caribbean and West Africa. While the Danish West Indies were under Danish control, Portuguese ships, traders, and historical precedents were a constant presence in the broader Atlantic economy.
